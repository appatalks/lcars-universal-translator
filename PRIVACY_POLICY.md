# Privacy Policy — LCARS Universal Translator

**Last updated: July 5, 2026**

## Overview

LCARS Universal Translator ("the App") is developed by appatalks. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data.

## Data Collection

**The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.**

### Speech & Audio
- Speech audio is processed on-device by Android's built-in SpeechRecognizer.
- If "Enhanced" (online) recognition is enabled, audio may be sent to Google's speech recognition servers per Google's own privacy policy. You can disable this by switching to "Offline" mode in Settings.
- Audio is never recorded, stored, or transmitted by the App itself.

### Translation
- Text translation is performed entirely on-device using Google ML Kit's offline translation models.
- No translated text is sent to any server.

### Bluetooth
- The App connects to paired Bluetooth audio devices for hands-free use.
- No Bluetooth device information is collected or transmitted.

### In-App Purchases
- The optional Pro Supporter purchase is processed entirely through Google Play Billing.
- The App does not collect or store any payment information. All billing is handled by Google Play per Google's privacy policy.

## Data Storage

The App stores only your preferences (language selections, audio settings) locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.

## Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

- **Google ML Kit** (on-device translation): https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms
- **Android SpeechRecognizer** (speech-to-text): Governed by your device manufacturer and Google's privacy policy
- **Google Play Billing** (in-app purchases): https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the App is suitable for all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

Any changes will be posted here with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
